## The two-party problem, handled honestly

Contracts have two signers, and they're rarely at the same keyboard. Three flows work:

- **Same room:** both parties sign on one device (landlord–tenant at a handover, freelancer–client on a call with screen share).
- **Sign and send:** sign your line, leave theirs blank, and email the PDF. The other party can add their signature without printing anything using the free [eSign PDF tool](https://ultimatetools.io/tools/pdf-tools/esign-pdf/) — same three signing modes, also free.
- **Paper preference:** leave both lines blank and the PDF prints exactly like a traditional contract.

One honest note: this stamps a signature *image* onto the document — the same thing you'd produce with a printer and a scanner, minus the printer and the scanner. It is not a certified digital-signature service with identity verification and audit trails. Simple signature images are widely used and generally recognized for everyday agreements like these, but if a deal is high-stakes, ask a lawyer what your jurisdiction requires. (The templates themselves are general-purpose starting points, not legal advice.)
